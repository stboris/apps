Knit Calc: Increase Evenly

Decrease, Gauge & Crochet

Spread increases and decreases evenly across a row or round, with written instructions you can follow stitch by stitch.

Increase or decrease evenly across a row or round. Enter your stitch count and how many stitches to add or remove, and get clear written instructions with a stitch-count check.

What it does

  • Increase evenly and decrease evenly
  • Flat or in the round
  • Written pattern like "*K6, M1, [K7, M1] ×6; rep from * once more (110 sts)"
  • Step-by-step list and a stitch-count check
  • Copy instructions with one tap
  • Dark mode and Dynamic Type

Free

  • Knitting increases (M1) and decreases (K2tog), flat and in the round, unlimited

PRO (one-time $0.99, no subscription, no ads)

  • Crochet mode in US terms: sc, 2 sc in next st, sc2tog
  • Choose your increase: M1, YO or KFB (KFB uses a stitch, the math handles it), or SSK for decreases
  • Gauge converter: rescale stitch and row counts to your gauge
  • Cast-on calculator: width × gauge
  • Multiple saved row counters

Pro is a single in-app purchase with Family Sharing.

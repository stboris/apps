Knitting & Crochet Calculator

Row Counter, Gauge & Increase

A free row counter for every project, plus increase-evenly instructions you can follow stitch by stitch.

Row counter, increase-evenly calculator and gauge tools for knitting and crochet, in one offline app.

What it does

  • Row counters for every project piece, saved automatically, with big tap targets and haptics
  • Increase or decrease evenly across a row or round, with written instructions like "*K6, M1, [K7, M1] ×6; rep from * once more (110 sts)"
  • See every increase marked on the round, plus a step-by-step list and a stitch-count check
  • Copy instructions with one tap
  • Dark mode and Dynamic Type

Free

  • Unlimited row counters, for knitting and crochet
  • Increase and decrease evenly for knitting (M1 and K2tog), flat and in the round

PRO (one-time purchase, no subscription, no ads)

  • Crochet shaping in US terms: sc, 2 sc in next st, sc2tog
  • Choose your stitch: M1, YO or KFB, or SSK for decreases
  • Gauge converter: rescale stitch and row counts to your gauge
  • Cast-on calculator: width × gauge

Pro is a single in-app purchase with Family Sharing.

Support

Restoring Pro: open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with. Pro is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Your data: everything stays on your iPhone. The app has no account and makes no network requests, so there is nothing to sync or delete on a server.

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to boris.stiner@gmail.com. Please mention the app name and your iOS version.

Epoxy Resin Calculator & Ratio

River Table, Floor & Mold Pour

Know exactly how much epoxy to mix before you open the kit: river tables, garage floors, countertops, molds and coasters.

Epoxy resin calculator for river tables, floors, countertops, molds and coasters. Enter the dimensions and get the exact amount of resin and hardener to mix.

What it does

  • River table (from narrowest and widest gap), rectangle, circle and flood coat
  • Floor & countertop mode: area in ft² or m² × coat thickness
  • Waste allowance (10% by default)
  • Results in ml, litres, US fl oz and US gallons
  • Mix ratios by volume: 1:1, 2:1, 3:1
  • Inches, centimetres or millimetres
  • Live drawing of your pour; dark mode and Dynamic Type

Free

  • Every shape including floor & countertop
  • All units
  • Volume ratios 1:1, 2:1 and 3:1

PRO (one-time purchase, no subscription, no ads)

  • Mix by weight (for example 100:45) with an adjustable density
  • Custom ratios from your resin's datasheet
  • Cost of the pour from kit price and size, and kits needed
  • Pour planner: max depth per layer gives the number of layers and volume per layer
  • Saved projects

Film Light Meter: Reciprocity

Long Exposure & ND Calculator

Meter with your camera, then correct long film exposures for reciprocity failure using the manufacturers' own datasheets.

Light meter and reciprocity calculator for film photographers. Point your phone at the scene, pick your aperture and film, and get the exposure, already corrected for reciprocity failure from the manufacturer's datasheet.

What it does

  • Light meter: reads the scene through the camera, shows EV and the shutter time for your aperture and film ISO; tap to hold a reading
  • Calibrate once against a meter you trust with exposure compensation
  • 30 black & white, colour negative and slide films, with corrections from official datasheets only and the source shown for every film
  • Manual metered-time input from 1/1000 s to hours
  • Corrected time, stops added and the datasheet note
  • Clear warnings when a time is beyond the datasheet's range
  • Dark by default for night work

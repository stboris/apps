Reciprocity: Film Exposure

Long Exposure & ND Timer

Correct long film exposures for reciprocity failure using the manufacturers' own datasheet figures, with ND stacking and a timer.

Reciprocity failure calculator for film photographers. Pick your film, enter the metered time, and get the corrected exposure based on the manufacturer's published datasheet.

What it does

  • 30 black & white, colour negative and slide films, grouped by manufacturer and searchable
  • Corrections from official datasheets only, with the source shown for every film
  • Metered time picker from 1/1000 s to hours
  • Corrected time, stops added and the datasheet note
  • Clear warnings when a time is beyond the datasheet's range
  • Dark by default for night work

Free

  • All built-in films and the corrected exposure for any metered time

PRO (one-time $0.99, no subscription, no ads)

  • ND filter stacking (stops or factors like ND1000), applied before reciprocity
  • Exposure countdown timer with a notification when it ends
  • Favourite films
  • Custom films with your own exponent or table

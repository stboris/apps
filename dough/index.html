Pizza dough calculator with baker's percentages, sourdough starter, poolish and biga, plus a yeast converter.

Set how many dough balls you want and how heavy each should be, pick your hydration, salt and yeast, and get exact grams of flour, water, salt and yeast instantly. Results update as you type.

What it does

  • Dough calculator using baker's percentages (hydration, salt, yeast, oil, sugar)
  • Presets: Neapolitan, New York, Detroit, Roman teglia, Sourdough and Focaccia
  • Sourdough starter (levain): exact split between starter and final dough
  • Yeast converter: instant dry, active dry and fresh yeast
  • Grams or ounces, comma or dot decimals
  • Dark mode and Dynamic Type
